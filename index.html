THE PROPERTY

A Munger landmark, run differently

Arya Plaza sits on an 80-foot stretch of Purabsarai Road at J.P. Chowk — one of Munger's most established commercial lanes, with shops, stores, and offices running on both sides. The ground floor is fully commercial: seven units, each with genuine street frontage and daily footfall.

What sets it apart isn't the address alone — it's how it's run. Every deal, every rupee, every rent revision is tracked on Arya Spaces, a purpose-built digital ledger, so tenants and the owner see the exact same numbers, always.

  • Front-road and back-road access on the same property
  • Dedicated storage and loft space in every unit
