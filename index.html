THE PROPERTY

A Munger landmark, run differently

Arya Plaza sits on Purabsarai Road at J.P. Chowk — one of Munger's most established commercial lanes, with shops, stores, and offices on both sides. Each unit has genuine street frontage and daily footfall, with a connected personal street running front road to back road.

What sets it apart isn't the address alone — it's how it's run. Every deal, every rupee, every rent revision is tracked on Arya Spaces, a purpose-built digital ledger, so tenants and the owner see the exact same numbers, always.

  • Front & side entry shutters, dual road access
  • Extra storage / godown space on top of every unit
  • 10×10 ft open space in front for parking or display
  • First-floor 2BHK residential space in development
